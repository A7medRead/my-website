The first screen the team sees every morning. Deliverability score, inbox rate, healthy servers, and active campaigns are pulled into one view instead of five separate tools — with a live alert triage panel so a critical issue is visible within seconds, not discovered hours later in a report.

The performance chart tracks delivery rate against bounce rate over the trailing week, which is usually the fastest way to spot a server or domain starting to degrade before it becomes a full outage.
