Automated inbox and spam placement tests run on a schedule across sending servers and seed mailboxes. Results are broken down by server, with inbox, spam, dropped, and ambiguous outcomes available for follow-up.

This is the system referenced in the [Log post on why emails land in spam](/log/why-emails-land-in-spam) — the automated version of the seed testing described there.
